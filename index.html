Everything you love.
Together.

One calm, native interface for everything you follow. Move effortlessly from discovering a new series to inspecting chapters and curating your personal collection.

Made for the way
you enjoy stories.

Every medium has its own rhythm. Ito adapts to each one—from page-by-page manga spreads and continuous webtoons to light novels and full-screen anime.

Read naturally.

Crisp right-to-left or left-to-right spreads for manga, alongside a dedicated light novel reader with customizable typography and spacing.

Uninterrupted flow.

Continuous vertical scrolling for webtoons and manhwa, with minimal floating controls that vanish the moment you immerse yourself in the artwork.

Watch natively.

Stream anime episodes in a clean, native iOS video player. Choose your stream quality, switch audio tracks, and customize subtitles without leaving the story.

Track with AniList, MyAnimeList or MangaBaka.

Sign in to AniList, MyAnimeList or MangaBaka and keep your reading and watching progress in sync automatically.

Make it yours with plugins.

Ito is an open client. Install modular .ito plugins from repositories you trust to bring your preferred anime, manga, and novel sources together.
